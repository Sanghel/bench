Design system · v0.3 · Ink × Cobalt
bench.
Developer tools that never leave your browser.

Ink does the work, cobalt points. Primary actions are black on light and white on dark. Cobalt is only for focus, selection, active navigation and links.

Green, red and amber are reserved for meaning: valid/added, error/removed, warning.
